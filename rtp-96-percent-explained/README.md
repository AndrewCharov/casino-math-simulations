# RTP Explained: What 96% RTP Really Means for Your $100

RTP explained with 10,000 simulated slot sessions: on a 96% RTP slot, the typical player got back only 83% after 100 spins, and $100 lasted 691 spins.

## Key results

- RTP is the share of <strong>all money bet</strong> that's paid back over a huge number of spins, not your deposit and not your session.
- On a 96% slot, the typical player's $100 lasted <strong>691 spins</strong>: about $700 of bets, then it was gone.
- After 100 spins, the typical player got back only <strong>83%</strong>, and <strong>27%</strong> were ahead.
- After 1,000,000 spins, every session landed between <strong>94% and 98%</strong> and nobody was ahead.
- At $1 a spin and 600 spins an hour: 96% RTP costs about <strong>$24/hour</strong>, 88% RTP costs <strong>$72/hour</strong>.
- $100 lasted <strong>691 spins at 96% RTP</strong> but only <strong>429 spins at 88%</strong>.
