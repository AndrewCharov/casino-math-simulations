# Betting Systems Tested on 70,000 Players: Which One Fails Fastest?

7 betting systems tested on 70,000 simulated roulette players: Labouchere broke 96% of players, flat betting just 1.8%. None beat the 2.7% house edge.

## Key results

- 7 betting systems, 10,000 players each: <strong>70,000 players</strong> on European roulette with a $1,000 bankroll.
- Fastest to fail: <strong>Labouchere</strong>, with 96% of players broke and the typical player out after <strong>67 spins</strong>.
- Safest: <strong>flat betting</strong>, with only <strong>1.8%</strong> broke after 1,000 spins.
- Most players ahead: <strong>Fibonacci at 24.5%</strong>, while flat betting had only 18.4%.
- Every system lost <strong>2.5% to 2.8%</strong> of all money bet: the 2.7% house edge, unchanged.
- Flat players bet about $10,000 and lost <strong>$272</strong> on average. D'Alembert players bet about $25,000 and lost <strong>$685</strong>.
