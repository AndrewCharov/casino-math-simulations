# European vs American Roulette: Why One Extra Zero Costs You Double

European vs American roulette, simulated: the typical $100 lasted 3,164 spins on a European wheel but only 1,742 on an American one. Roulette odds explained.

## Key results

- House edge: <strong>2.70%</strong> on a European wheel (37 pockets), <strong>5.26%</strong> on an American wheel (38 pockets), <strong>7.69%</strong> on a triple-zero wheel.
- Betting $1 on red until broke, the typical $100 lasted <strong>3,164 spins</strong> in Europe, <strong>1,742</strong> in America and <strong>1,230</strong> on triple zero.
- After 1,000 spins, <strong>18%</strong> of European players were ahead, versus <strong>4.6%</strong> of American players and <strong>0.7%</strong> on triple zero.
- Broke after 1,000 spins: <strong>1.8%</strong> European, <strong>9%</strong> American, <strong>28%</strong> triple zero.
- Average loss per 100 spins of $1: <strong>$2.74</strong> European, <strong>$5.23</strong> American, <strong>$7.64</strong> triple zero, almost exactly the house edge.
